About Me

I am a research scientist at the Ultra-Intelligent Computing/Communication Laboratory in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Chung-Ang University, Seoul, South Korea. My research focuses on cutting-edge technologies in machine learning, semantic communication, and ubiquitous computing.

With a strong background in computer science and engineering, I have contributed to numerous international publications and have received recognition for my work, including Excellent Paper Awards and Best Paper Awards at prestigious conferences.
